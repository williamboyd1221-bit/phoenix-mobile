# Phoenix Mobile

*Phoenix Mobile Advertising: ad workflows for small businesses in Oregon towns.*

We set up the same class of tools the big platforms use, for local businesses in **Eugene, Springfield, Salem, Portland and Roseburg**. We are not affiliated with Google, Meta or OpenAI.

## Principles

- **Provable.** Every ad ties to a page or offer you can open.
- **Clickable.** Links get tested before they ship.
- **No fakes.** No invented testimonials, stats or results. Example data is labeled *example*.
- **AI education.** Owners see how each step works.
- **Human approval.** Nothing is scheduled until a person says yes.

## The ad workflow

```
brief -> brand context -> static ad + short video script -> human approval gate -> scheduled post
```

1. **Brief.** Business, town, offer, and the one thing the ad should get someone to do.
2. **Brand context.** The business's own colors, voice and photos, kept apart from Phoenix's.
3. **Drafts.** One static ad and one short video script (about 15 seconds), plus a caption.
4. **Approval gate.** The business owner and a Phoenix reviewer both sign off. No sign-off, no post.
5. **Scheduled post.** Queued at a chosen time, and logged so the result can be checked later.

See [workflows/ad-workflow.md](workflows/ad-workflow.md) for the full example, including the five town variants and an example YAML brief.

## Phoenix ad style

Paper `#f4efe6`, ink `#12100e`, ember `#e36a2e` as the single accent. Newsreader serif headlines, a circular monogram, plenty of paper. The call to action carries no price.

## CONCEPT: can-collection community benefit

> **This is a concept, not a launched service.** It has no partners, no numbers and no start date.

The idea: a local business sponsors a can and bottle collection drive in its town, and Phoenix promotes the drive inside that business's ad campaign. The ad gives people a reason to show up and gives the business a way to be part of the community. Phoenix would handle the posts and the sign-off flow. How a drive is run and where the cans go would be decided with the community before anything is announced.

Details and an example ad outline: [workflows/ad-workflow.md](workflows/ad-workflow.md#concept-can-collection-community-benefit).
